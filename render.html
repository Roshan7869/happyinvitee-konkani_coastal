our story

  1. 2021

    One umbrella, two people

    A cloudburst on the way to the bus stop, one umbrella between strangers, and a red-mud road neither of us wanted to end.

  2. 2024

    Sunsets on the boat

    Every Sunday on his uncle's fishing boat, pulled up on the sand, arguing about whose family makes the better sol kadi.

  3. 2026

    The yes

    At a village feast, over banana leaves. He asked between the rice and the payasam; she said yes with her mouth full.
